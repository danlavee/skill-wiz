# Repair

Reached only from a deficiency in a protocol-valid run — a failed gate or a
short grade — never as an entry point. Produces a verified owner-complete
transaction; control returns to `run-and-grade.md`.

## Record the fault

Identify every work model, instruction owner, or evaluation gate that permitted the fault, on the subject side and the Wiz side; do not stop at the nearest wording or symptom. Record the observed fault and its evidence, the candidate causal mechanism, the attempted falsifier, the base candidate's logged grades, and prior supported occurrences.

A material observation that remains pending or unverifiable under the contradiction states in `run-and-grade.md` cannot authorize subject repair or subject-defect attribution; route it only as evaluation-process evidence until resolved.

## Converge on the cause

Unmask the Worker that produced the deliverable, give it the exact defect
evidence, and audit its decision trace: which missing rule permitted the path it
chose? Challenge each answer until it converges. An answer has converged when it
satisfies Invariant-Level Instruction, Act Over Artifact, and Level-Locality, and
is:

- **Load-bearing**: accounting for at least one observation beyond the one that produced it;
- **Stable**: surviving a further challenge without semantic change.

Push each challenge downward, toward what permitted the decision, never
outward toward what else resembles the failure. Disclose no candidate invariant,
grading, or intended repair; a question that supplies the answer's form
manufactures it instead of testing it. Stop when convergence holds, or when
successive turns stop changing the answer without reaching it — record that as an
unconverged cause and repair nothing on it. Do not edit the subject before
convergence, and apply only the converged invariant.

## Form the transaction

Map every affected responsibility to its authoritative owner and bind the edits as one immutable owner-complete transaction. Route every frozen finding exactly once into a conserved obligation ledger disposing each as subject control, target or evaluation control, process or runtime control, or unresolved; assign every supported obligation one control and one owner, and identify every exact replacement and superseded deletion. Do not let evidence disappear because it is unsuitable for the subject skill, and do not use a local wording edit to stand in for a cross-boundary repair.

Act Over Artifact binds the recorded mechanism; Removing Choice Over Adding Checks binds each control. Follow the target-native and portability rules in `build.md` for any wording that enters a subject skill, and revalidate structure there after applying.

The transaction is incomplete until executed evidence on the exact candidate demonstrates each claimed mechanism interruption: the fault's own case moving, and no other case's grade falling.

## Gate the transition

Before full dispatch of a locked repair transition, run the anchor against the candidate and compare every grade to what the log holds for the base it was built from. A grade that falls rejects the transition regardless of gains elsewhere. Reach is read from what runs outside the fault's own case do, which the log records in prose beside their grades; a control that changes nothing there is rejected under Removing Choice Over Adding Checks. Repeat the complete comparison once on the unchanged candidate before any stability or generalization claim.

For an exploratory trial, rerun only its recorded case.

## Verify independently

Before applying, rerunning, or recommending a repair as complete, dispatch a Repair Verifier as authorized by the autonomy contract, providing the authoritative sources, fault evidence, causal record, conserved obligation ledger, exact owner-complete transaction, canary evidence, and evaluation design.

The Verifier first applies the act-over-artifact test to the recorded mechanism itself: a mechanism stated as a property the deliverable should have had is `fail`, however well its control interrupts it — a control cannot rise above the altitude of the cause it answers. It records the reach and force of each control: one that changes nothing on runs that never exhibited the fault is `fail`, and one whose satisfaction the acting agent adjudicates is `fail`, whatever the wording of either. It then records `pass`, `fail`, or `unverifiable` for every frozen obligation, each disposition citing the preserved finding, authority and owner, old-model counterexample, executed negative falsifier, observed new-model rejection, the logged grades compared against, the movement observed, superseded removal, and residual uncertainty. It reconciles the ledger against all frozen findings and may add an omitted obligation. Any completion shortcut excluded by the transaction contract, or any disposition without executed exact-candidate evidence, is `unverifiable`.

Any `fail` or `unverifiable` blocks completion. Revise the transaction and repeat with a fresh Verifier. Wiz may supply missing evidence but cannot act as verifier or override the result, and this role neither replaces nor joins the Judge review.

## Apply and present

Apply the autonomy contract to verifier dispatch, presentation, and application. When prior review is required, present the exact transaction, causal record, Verifier result, uncertainty, and verification plan, then wait. Otherwise apply the reversible transaction, emitting rather than applying any edit whose owner is an accepted anchor or a stored case, and retain its exact diff for the next material checkpoint. Re-evaluate any material deviation affecting the semantic contract, owner map, or a User-owned boundary before continuing.

After the gate passes, record the evidence-based conclusion and recommendation. Preserve locked conditions throughout. Claim no generalization from repair cases.

## Retain

Retain the frozen findings, obligation ledger, causal mechanism and its convergence record, falsifier and result, the grade comparison against the base, owner map, exact transaction, control evidence, residual uncertainty, and Verifier record.
