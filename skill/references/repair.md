# Repair

Reached only from a deficiency in a protocol-valid run — a failed gate or a
short grade — never as an entry point, and never after a User gate on that
deficiency alone. Produces a verified owner-complete transaction or an explicit
blocked or unconverged stop; the User gate on this path is adopt or apply of
that package. After apply, control returns to `run-and-grade.md`.

## Record the fault

Identify every work model, instruction owner, or evaluation gate that permitted the fault, on the subject side and the Wiz side; do not stop at the nearest wording or symptom. Record the observed fault and its evidence, the candidate causal mechanism, the attempted falsifier, the base candidate's logged grades, and prior supported occurrences.

A material observation that remains pending or unverifiable under the contradiction states in `run-and-grade.md` cannot authorize subject repair or subject-defect attribution; route it only as evaluation-process evidence until resolved.

## Converge on the cause

Unmask the Worker that produced the deliverable, give it the exact defect
evidence, and audit its decision trace: which missing rule permitted the path it
chose? Challenge each answer until it converges. An answer has converged when it
satisfies Invariant-Level Instruction, Act Over Artifact, and Level-Locality, and
is:

- **Load-bearing**: accounting for at least one observation beyond the one that produced it;
- **Stable**: surviving a further challenge without semantic change.

Push each challenge downward, toward what permitted the decision, never
outward toward what else resembles the failure. Disclose no candidate invariant,
grading, or intended repair; a question that supplies the answer's form
manufactures it instead of testing it. Stop when convergence holds, or when
successive turns stop changing the answer without reaching it — record that as an
unconverged cause and repair nothing on it. Do not edit the subject before
convergence, and apply only the converged invariant.

## Prepare controls and bind the transaction

**Change Request Form** and **Transaction Form** are different steps. Change Request
Form produces exact package/process spans and runs Proposal Admission
(`proposal-admission.md`). Transaction Form binds the owner-complete edit set after
an admissible control exists.

Before binding Transaction Form edits:
1. Freeze the residual ledger.
2. State each explicit control. When a control is a package or process Change
   Request, complete Proposal Admission with Dispose outcome **Admit** and User
   acceptance of the spans before descent. Dispose **Admit** alone does not start
   descent. A Guide bind may select **Admit** among disagreeing Review
   dispositions; Guide bind is not itself **Admit**.
3. Run the descent test in `descent.md` on every proposed control that is ready
   for descent. Only an admissible (`pass`) control may enter Gate, Verify,
   re-run, or apply. A `partial` result stays on the same ledger: refine that
   proposal and re-test. A `fail` or reframe is not tested on this ledger. The
   descent test applies to this transaction itself, including process-file edits.
   Do not run descent on a Change Request that has not yet reached **Admit** and
   User acceptance of the spans.

**Suggest the actual fix explicitly.** Each proposed control is a concrete
suggestion, not only a causal story. For every obligation the transaction
claims, state at least: authoritative owner path; exact replacement, addition,
or deletion (diff or equivalent wording); which residual freedom that control
removes; and what evidence would leave residual open vs closed. A ledger that
names only mechanisms, tenets, or intent without those fields is incomplete —
refine under the same residual ledger until the suggestion is explicit, or
record unconverged and repair nothing. Do not Gate on an inexplicit proposal.

Repair owns cause convergence, residual ledger, descent, and Repair Verification
for failure-led transactions; it does not own Proposal Admission procedure.

Map every affected responsibility to its authoritative owner and bind the edits as one immutable owner-complete transaction. Route every frozen finding exactly once into a conserved obligation ledger disposing each as subject control, target or evaluation control, process or runtime control, or unresolved; assign every supported obligation one control and one owner, and identify every exact replacement and superseded deletion. Do not let evidence disappear because it is unsuitable for the subject skill, and do not use a local wording edit to stand in for a cross-boundary repair.

Act Over Artifact binds the recorded mechanism; Removing Choice Over Adding Checks binds each control. Follow the target-native and portability rules in `build.md` for any wording that enters a subject skill, and revalidate structure there after applying. Apply the same altitude tests to any Wiz-side instruction edit in the transaction and to any package edit presented as the recommendation (`SKILL.md`, Separate fact from judgment).

When the work repairs a skill under a governing package, for each permission class a subject control addresses, dispose that class on the governor in the same transaction: by a control there, or by a recorded finding that the class is absent that states what was examined. Completing the named skill alone leaves the transaction incomplete.

A disposition that claims a permission class is closed on a side, or that a side needs no control, is incomplete until a party that did not produce that disposition settles it (Independent Repair Verification). That party records only residual freedom still open on each claimed side (what may still be treated as enough to stop), or closed, citing the control that removes that freedom. The producer of the subject control or of the absence claim is not that party. Naming related rules without removing the freedom under review is not closed. In this process that party is the Repair Verifier under Verify independently (or another non-producing role under the same rules).

Wiz (or whoever dispatches) authors the settler's brief under Stance Neutrality: the brief states the permission class under review, the sides and claims to settle, and the closure rules above; it must not instruct the settler to prefer residual or clearance, to find fault, or to confirm soundness. The brief carries the settlement rules the settler must apply—not a full core-tenet recitation, and not Stance Neutrality restated as the settler's self-policed attitude. Dumping every core tenet into the brief is not required and often injects preferred-outcome nuance.

The brief must also carry every **object settlement condition** that distinguishes residual open from residual closed for the claims under review (for example: a named freedom remains available on a path → residual open; a cited control removes that freedom → residual closed). Those conditions are criteria on the package under review, not preferred terminals. Omitting a condition the fault or twin requires is an incomplete brief; Stance Neutrality does not license the omission. Preferring residual or clearance remains forbidden.

**Completion is blocked while residual under review is open.** A producer ledger, deliverable note, or package edit that claims multi-side or governor disposition complete does not complete the transaction. Only a non-producing settler's residual-closed dispositions on every claimed side, plus the executed interruption evidence below, may complete. Residual open, fail, or unverifiable on any claimed side leaves the transaction incomplete; do not recommend apply or evolution.

The transaction is incomplete until executed evidence on the exact candidate demonstrates each claimed mechanism interruption: the fault's own case moving, and no other case's grade falling.

## Gate the transition

Do not open this gate for a control that lacks a current descent `pass` under
`descent.md` on the frozen residual ledger for this fault. Descent `partial` or
`fail` is not a gate entry.

Before full dispatch of a locked repair transition, run the anchor against the candidate and compare every grade to what the log holds for the base it was built from. A grade that falls rejects the transition regardless of gains elsewhere. Reach is read from what runs outside the fault's own case do, which the log records in prose beside their grades; a control that changes nothing there is rejected under Removing Choice Over Adding Checks. Repeat the complete comparison once on the unchanged candidate before any stability or generalization claim.

For an exploratory trial, rerun only its recorded case.

## Verify independently

Before applying, rerunning, or recommending a repair as complete, dispatch a Repair Verifier as authorized by the autonomy contract. Author the brief under Stance Neutrality. It carries only what settlement requires: authoritative package surfaces to examine, the permission class under review, the owner-complete transaction and multi-side residual claims (including that a side needs no control), fault evidence and canary material needed to test those claims, and the settlement rules in Prepare controls and bind the transaction (residual open or closed with a cited control; naming related rules without removing the freedom is not closed; producer narrative is not evidence). Do not load the brief with a preferred terminal (find residual, confirm clearance) or with a dump of every core tenet. Do not tell the Verifier to "be stance-neutral"; satisfy Stance Neutrality by how the brief is written.

The Verifier first applies the act-over-artifact test to the recorded mechanism itself: a mechanism stated as a property the deliverable should have had is `fail`, however well its control interrupts it — a control cannot rise above the altitude of the cause it answers. It records the reach and force of each control: one that changes nothing on runs that never exhibited the fault is `fail`, and one whose satisfaction the acting agent adjudicates is `fail`, whatever the wording of either. It then records `pass`, `fail`, or `unverifiable` for every frozen obligation, each disposition citing the preserved finding, authority and owner, old-model counterexample, executed negative falsifier, observed new-model rejection, the logged grades compared against, the movement observed, superseded removal, and residual uncertainty. For each multi-side residual claim it settles residual freedom still open or closed with a cited control that removes that freedom; producer narrative alone is not evidence; naming related tenets without that removal is not closed. It reconciles the ledger against all frozen findings and may add an omitted obligation. Any completion shortcut excluded by the transaction contract, or any disposition without executed exact-candidate evidence, is `unverifiable`.

Any `fail` or `unverifiable` blocks completion. Residual open on any multi-side claim under review is `fail` for completion even when subject control is sound. Revise the transaction and repeat with a fresh Verifier. Wiz may supply missing evidence but cannot act as verifier or override the result, and this role neither replaces nor joins the Judge review.

Retain the settler's brief exactly as dispatched with the Verifier record. A preferred terminal in that brief is process evidence under `evaluation-design.md`, not a successful settlement.

## Apply and present

This path owns the first User-facing stop after a deficient run. The decision-ready package is the run's dispositions and grades, the convergence record or explicit unconverged cause, the obligation ledger, the **explicit suggested fixes** (owner, exact edit, residual removed) when a transaction exists, the Verifier result or the block that prevented verification, residual uncertainty, and the recommendation (adopt, apply, or stop). An unconverged cause or a Verifier `fail` or `unverifiable` that cannot be cleared still presents — repair nothing, and carry that outcome in the package. Do not present residual-open as a completed repair. Do not present mechanism-only RCA as the User-facing fix suggestion.

Apply the autonomy contract to verifier dispatch, presentation, and application. When prior review is required, present that package, then wait. Otherwise apply the reversible transaction, emitting rather than applying any edit whose owner is an accepted anchor or a stored case, and retain its exact diff for the next material checkpoint. Re-evaluate any material deviation affecting the semantic contract, owner map, or a User-owned boundary before continuing.

After the gate passes and any authorized apply completes, record the evidence-based conclusion and return to `run-and-grade.md` on the resulting candidate for the **mandatory re-run of the fault's falsifying case** (and any process case the ledger obligated). A repair or self-evolution claim is incomplete until that re-run shows the removed freedom is no longer an available stop and no protected grade falls. Preserve locked conditions throughout. Claim no generalization from repair cases.

## Retain

Retain the frozen findings, obligation ledger, causal mechanism and its convergence record, falsifier and result, the grade comparison against the base, owner map, exact transaction, control evidence, residual uncertainty, and Verifier record. Retain the residual ledger snapshot and each descent disposition (`pass` / `partial` / `fail`) with the proposal text tested, per `descent.md`.
