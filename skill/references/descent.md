# Residual descent

Owner of which repair proposals may enter Gate, Verify, re-run, or apply
transition — not of cause convergence (owned by `repair.md`) and not of residual
settlement after a run (also `repair.md`). Read completely before admitting a
control to the expensive path.

A control that is a package or process Change Request must already have reached
**Admit** under `proposal-admission.md` and User acceptance of the spans before
D1–D4. Descent does not run Proposal Admission.

This file does not claim the search walks to a global fixed point. It removes the
freedom to spend that path on steps that are not residual descent on a frozen
measure, and it requires partial results to refine under the same measure rather
than open a new story.

## Freeze the residual ledger

Before proposing a control, freeze:

1. **Permission class** — what may still be treated as enough to stop.
2. **Object settlement conditions** — what leaves residual open vs closed on
   each claimed side (same sense as in `repair.md` settler briefs).
3. **Sides and open residuals** — which freedoms are still open under those
   conditions, cited from evidence, not from a new narrative.

A change of permission class or of those conditions is a **reframe**. A reframe
is not a step on this ledger: open or extend a case under
`evaluation-design.md`. Do not admit a reframe as a repair transition of the
prior residual.

## Descent test (eligibility)

The unit under test is an **explicit control suggestion** (owner path, exact
edit or equivalent, residual freedom named) — see Prepare controls and bind the transaction in
`repair.md`. Mechanism prose alone is not a proposal; score it `partial` or
`fail` until the suggestion is concrete enough to apply or reject.

A proposed control is **admissible** for Gate / Verify / re-run / apply only
when all of the following hold. Record the result as `pass`, `partial`, or
`fail` against this ledger (producer records; non-producing settlement of
completion remains under `repair.md`).

| # | Criterion | Pass | Fail |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| D1 | **Same coordinates** | Targets only residuals on the frozen ledger | Reframes class or conditions; invents a parallel residual without casing it |
| D2 | **Named freedom** | Names a still-open residual and a control that removes that freedom | Names tenet titles, notes, or “look harder” without removing a free path |
| D3 | **Stricter prediction** | States what the next falsifier re-run must show if the control holds | No predicted change to the stop, or only a prettier package |
| D4 | **Path removal** | Changes which paths exist (Removing Choice); not only package growth or a self-adjudicated attitude | Checklist, self-scored “gradient,” or annotation after the decision |

**pass** — all of D1–D4 hold → may enter Gate (then Verify, re-run, apply per `repair.md`).  
**fail** — D1 fails, or the proposal is only reframe/annotation → **do not test** on this ledger; case the reframe or drop the proposal.  
**partial** — D1 holds and the proposal aims at an open residual, but D2–D4 are incomplete or weak (force unclear, prediction underspecified, path not yet removed) → **do not invent a new mechanism**. Refine the same proposal under the same ledger and re-run this test. Partial is not admission and not a license to sideways-search.

The descent test applies to process and package controls, including changes to
this file and to `repair.md`. A suggestion that fails its own D1–D4 is not
admissible until refined to pass (or dropped).

## After a tested step

- Re-run fails while descent registration was honest → that control is
  **falsified** on this ledger; residual stays open; next proposal must account
  for the failed step (load-bearing on that observation), not ignore it for a
  fresh story.
- Residual closed under settlement and Falsifier Closure → ledger entry may
  close; do not keep testing closed residuals.
- No admissible refinement remains → record **unconverged** or blocked under
  `repair.md`; repair nothing further on this ledger until new evidence reopens
  it through Run/Cases.

## Not this owner

- Converging the causal answer (interrogation) — `repair.md`.
- Settling residual after evidence — `repair.md` / Repair Verifier.
- Blind Worker briefs — `evaluation-design.md`.
- Claiming evolution complete — Falsifier Closure and `repair.md` Apply.

## Retain

Retain each frozen ledger snapshot used, each descent disposition
(`pass` / `partial` / `fail` with D1–D4 notes), and the proposal text tested.
