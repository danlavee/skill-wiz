**Discriminates**: width versus depth. The pivotal recurring failure.

**Repairs**: `test-resources/` — the diagram skill.

**Setup**: `produce.md` asks for a directed path with one edge that bypasses a
hop. The case qualifies when the defect belongs to a nameable category, so an
obvious universal phrasing of the wrong answer is available — "all
conventionally expected <category> must be present".

The run records order as an authority atom four times over — "first step of the
four-step sequence", then second, third, fourth — and paints none of it. The
audit's own row says what carries it: "order alone (drawn after R2/R3) carries
the sequence", meaning the order of elements in the source file. That is not a
painted channel, and the subject already requires that final paint reconstruct
each atom "without source knowledge". The audit closed `pass` on that row and on
all 57.

**Attractor**: climb from the instance to its category. The result names no
instance data, states a rule governing any execution under the contract, and
holds stable under challenge. It passes every form gate and remains an
artifact property.

**Grades**:

- *Reach* — how much changes on runs whose subject matter excludes the
  category, from nothing to all of them.
- *Locus* — what the repair governs, from what the Worker must consult or
  complete before it may stop, to what the deliverable must contain.

The attractor above phrases the rule to cover any execution and still sits at
*reach* nothing. Width of phrasing is not a dimension here: it moves
independently of both, and scoring it would score the symptom's width.
