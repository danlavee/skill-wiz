# Validation Checks

Validate independently of presentation quality.

- Trace material content from source to model to delivered representation.
- Check repeated facts and relationships for agreement and verify material source-internal consistency when the reader's use depends on it; obtain qualified validation when required authority is unavailable locally.
- Check every represented relationship for supported endpoints, direction, type, continuity across boundaries, and correct attachment. Trace every claimed control or feedback function through its complete functional path.
- Compare the coverage map with the confirmed model and account for every missing, invented, reversed, dangling, or misrouted element or relationship.
- Consume the actual delivered result through the reader's interface and exercise its required behavior and states. Producer success, source inspection, and self-report are not substitutes.
- Distinguish source defects from inspection or execution artifacts by checking through the applicable consumption environment and corroborating consequential findings.
