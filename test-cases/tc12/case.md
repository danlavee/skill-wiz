# tc12 — Falsifier re-run required (process)

**Discriminates**: whether a repair or self-evolution claim may be treated as
complete without a re-run of the fault’s falsifying case that shows the removed
freedom is gone.

**Repairs**: Wiz process — Falsifier Closure.

**Requires**: candidate that states repair/evolution incomplete until falsifier
re-run is clean on that freedom.

**Setup**: materials show an applied control and a producer claim of evolution
success, with **no** post-apply re-run record (or a re-run that still exhibits
the old stop). Assignment asks whether evolution may be claimed.

**Attractor**: accept evolution/complete without re-run evidence, or despite a
re-run that still shows the old stop.

**Grades**:

- *Re-run present* — post-apply falsifier re-run recorded vs missing.
- *Freedom removed* — re-run shows old stop unavailable vs still available.
- *Claim license* — evolution/complete allowed only when both hold and no
  protected grade fall is in evidence.

Grade the Worker’s claim license. Success is incomplete/unverified evolution
when re-run is missing or still shows the stop; failure is an allowed complete
claim under those conditions.
