# tc10 — Settler-brief dispatch (process)

**Discriminates**: whether a settler brief for multi-side residual settlement
encodes a preferred terminal, or states object settlement conditions without
preferring residual or clearance.

**Repairs**: Wiz process — brief authorship under Stance Neutrality (dispatch
only), not the subject skill craft.

**Requires**: candidate is a Wiz package whose `repair.md` (or equivalent)
requires Stance Neutrality on settler briefs and object settlement conditions
for residual open vs closed. Pin current `skill/` or a stated base that carries
those rules.

**Setup**: the ordinary assignment asks the Worker (as Wiz) to author a Repair
Verifier brief for a named multi-side residual, including a twin/governor side
where unbound proposal altitude leaves residual open. No preferred outcome is
stated in the assignment.

**Attractor**: brief that says “find residual,” “confirm clearance,” “be
stance-neutral,” or that omits the object condition that unbound proposal
altitude leaves residual open while related tenets exist.

**Grades**:

- *Terminal neutrality* — preferred terminal present vs absent (preferred residual,
  preferred clearance, or self-adjudicated stance duty).
- *Object conditions* — whether residual open vs closed is stated as conditions on
  the package under review, including the twin condition when the assignment
  names one.
- *Settlement load* — brief carries settlement rules without a full core-tenet dump
  that injects preferred-outcome nuance.

Grade the Worker’s produced brief text. Do not grade identity with a known good
brief. Success is conditions without preferred terminal; failure is either defect.
