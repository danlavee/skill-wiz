# Repair

Enter here with a deficiency to explain — a failed gate, a short grade, or an
observed fault reported without a trial. Produces a verified owner-complete
transaction; the successor entry is `run-and-grade.md`.

## Record the fault

Identify every work model, instruction owner, or evaluation gate that permitted the fault, on the subject side and the Wiz side; do not stop at the nearest wording or symptom. Record the observed fault and its evidence, the candidate causal mechanism, the attempted falsifier, the protected passes, and prior supported occurrences.

A material observation that remains pending or unverifiable under the contradiction states in `run-and-grade.md` cannot authorize subject repair or subject-defect attribution; route it only as evaluation-process evidence until resolved.

## Converge on the cause

Unmask the Worker that produced the deliverable, give it the exact defect
evidence, and audit its decision trace: which missing rule permitted the path it
chose? Challenge each answer until it converges. An answer has converged when it
satisfies Invariant-Level Instruction, Act Over Artifact, and Level-Locality, and
is:

- **Load-bearing**: accounting for at least one observation beyond the one that produced it;
- **Stable**: surviving a further challenge without semantic change.

Stability is unreachable in one turn, so the loop always runs at least two — and
stability alone is a stall, since a Worker certain of a symptom converges
fastest. Push each challenge downward, toward what permitted the decision, never
outward toward what else resembles the failure. Disclose no candidate invariant,
grading, or intended repair; a question that supplies the answer's form
manufactures it instead of testing it. Stop when convergence holds, or when
successive turns stop changing the answer without reaching it — record that as an
unconverged cause and repair nothing on it. Do not edit the subject before
convergence, and apply only the converged invariant.

## Form the transaction

Reconcile all settled and unresolved findings into a conserved obligation ledger, classifying each as subject control, target/evaluation control, process/runtime control, or unresolved. Do not let evidence disappear because it is unsuitable for the subject skill, and do not use a local wording edit to stand in for a cross-boundary repair.

Map every affected responsibility to its authoritative owner and bind the edits as one immutable owner-complete transaction. Route every frozen finding exactly once, assign every supported obligation one control and one owner, and identify every exact replacement and superseded deletion. Follow the target-native and portability rules in `build.md` for any wording that enters a subject skill, and revalidate structure there after applying.

The recorded mechanism must name an act, not a deliverable property, and each control must interrupt that mechanism rather than restate a desired result. Each control removes a choice; it does not add a check.

The transaction is incomplete until executed negative and protected-positive evidence on the exact candidate demonstrates each claimed mechanism interruption. Installed wording, structural validation, retained prior positives, case IDs alone, or a bare verifier verdict cannot complete it.

## Gate the transition

Before full dispatch of a locked repair transition, freeze the prior and candidate snapshots and run an exact-candidate protected canary binding the snapshot digests and each inherited protected case's content, input, expected dimension, evidence, protocol, grading, brief, and runtime digests. Every inherited protected case requires a fresh artifact, current-digest verification, the required independent Judges, and no unresolved contradiction. Any failure rejects the transition regardless of aggregate gains. Repeat the complete gate once on the unchanged candidate before any stability or generalization claim.

For an exploratory trial, rerun only its recorded case and make no generalization claim.

## Verify independently

Before applying, rerunning, or recommending a repair as complete, dispatch a Repair Verifier in a fresh context as authorized by the autonomy contract, providing the authoritative sources, fault evidence, causal record, conserved obligation ledger, exact owner-complete transaction, canary evidence, and evaluation design.

The Verifier first applies the act-over-artifact test to the recorded mechanism itself: a mechanism stated as a property the deliverable should have had is `fail`, however well its control interrupts it — a control cannot rise above the altitude of the cause it answers. It records the reach and force of each control: one that changes nothing on runs that never exhibited the fault is `fail`, and one whose satisfaction the acting agent adjudicates is `fail`, whatever the wording of either. It then records `pass`, `fail`, or `unverifiable` for every frozen obligation, each disposition citing the preserved finding, authority and owner, old-model counterexample, executed negative falsifier, observed new-model rejection, executed protected-positive control, observed preservation, superseded removal, and residual uncertainty. It reconciles the ledger against all frozen findings and may add an omitted obligation. Any completion shortcut excluded by the transaction contract, or any disposition without executed exact-candidate evidence, is `unverifiable`.

Any `fail` or `unverifiable` blocks completion. Revise the transaction and repeat with a fresh Verifier. Wiz may supply missing evidence but cannot act as verifier or override the result, and this role neither replaces nor joins the Judge review.

## Apply and present

Apply the autonomy contract to verifier dispatch, presentation, and application. When prior review is required, present the exact transaction, causal record, Verifier result, uncertainty, and verification plan, then wait. Otherwise apply the reversible transaction and retain its exact diff for the next material checkpoint. Re-evaluate any material deviation affecting the semantic contract, owner map, or a User-owned boundary before continuing.

After the gate passes, record the evidence-based conclusion and recommendation. Preserve locked conditions throughout. Never teach a failed Agent, reuse coached evidence, or claim generalization from repair cases.

## Retain

Retain the frozen findings, obligation ledger, causal mechanism and its convergence record, falsifier and result, protected passes, owner map, exact transaction, control evidence, residual uncertainty, and Verifier record.
