# Run and Grade

Enter here with a skill and an accepted evaluation record. Produces gate
dispositions and grades; any deficiency in either opens `repair.md`.

When an authorized record already covers the changed surface and its permissions
and conditions remain valid, freeze the changed subject and dispatch the next
prescribed run immediately after structural validation passes. Do not request
confirmation or substitute a progress report. Realign only when a governed
element no longer holds.

## Run blind work

Apply the brief gates owned by `evaluation-design.md` before dispatch.

Use a fresh Agent context holding only the assignment and permitted resources, in its own working location. Hold recorded conditions constant across comparable runs. Preserve the initial response, actions, artifacts, trace, and state before any follow-up. Do not coach a failed Agent, use coached work as evidence, or permit consequential action beyond the recorded boundary. Keep raw artifacts separate from Wiz-authored instructions.

When the record includes conversational review, send its fixed prompt as a separate turn after the initial result is preserved, never as part of the assignment. Use it to expose the Agent's goal model, account of work, experienced difficulty, completion judgment, and unresolved gaps. Permit no result change afterward and grade the reflection separately. Self-assessment is evidence about the Agent's model, not verification of the result, and it opens the interrogation loop rather than settling it.

## Interpret and grade

Grade only a protocol-valid first result. Record each gate disposition with the artifact, diagnostic, trace, or viewed surface establishing it. Assertions and aggregate verdicts are not evidence; a hard failure cannot be offset by quality elsewhere; an absent or unreviewed required surface cannot pass. Producer closure is a production claim only and closes no semantic, Judge, transition, or promotion authority.

When a graded property cannot be settled by a gate, dispatch independent Judges with identical briefs and resources — enough that a single dissent is visible rather than decisive, which sets the minimum at three. Each examines every delivered surface in the form the accepted result requires, comparing only the accepted desired result with the untouched first result, and accounts for the same obligation IDs citing authority, unit, digest, and localized evidence. The desired result is an exemplar only for mapped properties; ungraded content creates no deficiency. Wiz must not produce, repair, or complete the result. Preserve every review separately.

Verify hard conflicts directly and retain soft disagreement without voting. Only opposed factual observations enter the states `observed -> opposed -> replay-pending -> resolved | unverifiable`, replayed by fresh reviewers on the same artifact, authority, obligation, evidence, and digests without prior votes. A pending or unverifiable material contradiction blocks promotion and cannot authorize subject repair or settled subject-defect attribution; it may support evaluation-process RCA.

Record only decision-relevant observations, evidence, and uncertainty. Require no ordinal score the anchor has not defined, and no per-dimension alternative or recommendation.

## Decide and present

Record the evidence-based evaluation decision and recommendation. Present the evidence, interpretation, alternatives, and confidence. Final adoption remains a non-waivable User gate.

A failed gate or a short grade enters `repair.md`. Do not edit the subject here.

## Retain

Retain the exact assignment, resource manifest and Judge brief, the untouched first response and artifacts, the access trace and diagnostics, each separate Judge review, contradiction states, and final dispositions.
