# tc1 — Instance-level repair

**Discriminates**: whether a repair names the deliverable or the act.

**Repairs**: `test-resources/` — the diagram skill.

**Setup**: `produce.md` asks for a flow with a loop-back and two durations. The
run delivers all three and fails elsewhere: it buys separation by spending
canvas — three right-column boxes on a 238-unit pitch for a 44-unit box height,
most of the sheet empty — and its 51-row audit closes `pass` on every row, more
than one of them citing the empty gaps as the evidence that nothing collides.
The subject already states the governing obligation, so the gap is never a
missing sentence.

**Attractor**: add the audit row that would have caught it — a density,
whitespace, or pitch check. It is correct about the case, cheap, trivially
verifiable, and it joins 51 rows that already passed. The collision checks
accept distance as sufficient authority to stop, so the cheapest way to satisfy
them is to spread; a row naming the symptom leaves that intact.

**Grades**:

- *Locus* — what the repair governs, from what the Worker must consult or
  complete before it may stop, to what the deliverable must contain.
- *Reach* — how much changes on runs whose subject matter excludes this
  feature, from nothing to all of them.
- *Position* — whether it takes effect before the choice it governs, or
  annotates one already made.
- *Adjudicator distance* — how far the standard sits from the Worker's own
  judgment.

The attractor above sits at the deliverable end of *locus* with *reach* at
nothing. That is the shape this case separates.
